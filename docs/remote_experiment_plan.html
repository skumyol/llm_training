8. Paper-Ready Pass Criteria

  • Required: response eval has BERTScore, length ratio, repetition, gated and ungated leakage.
  • Required: predicted routing has prediction_coverage=1.0 or an explicit missing-prediction table.
  • Required: no zero-leakage claim unless gated and ungated denominators both support it.
  • Preferred: 3 seeds for Track A and Qwen latent headline comparisons.
  • Preferred: 200 human-eval items with 2 annotators.
  • Reject-risk: claiming MoE superiority without a parameter-matched dense GPT baseline.
