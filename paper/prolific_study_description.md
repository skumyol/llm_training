## Study Title
Label a fantasy-game dialogue dataset (150 turns, ~2.5 hours)

## Brief Description
We are researchers building AI systems for non-player character (NPC) dialogue in video games. We need human annotators to label 150 short dialogue turns on 8 social-state dimensions (e.g., emotional valence, trust level, conversational strategy).

You will read a scene description, a short dialogue history, a player utterance, and an NPC response, then select labels from dropdown menus. There are no "right" answers for subjective dimensions — we are measuring how humans naturally interpret social state in text.

**No special knowledge is required.** Native or fluent English is sufficient.

## What You'll Do
1. Open a web-based annotation interface (Gradio app).
2. Enter your Prolific ID as your annotator name.
3. Read each dialogue turn carefully.
4. Select one label for each of 8 heads from dropdown menus.
5. Click **Submit & Next** after each turn.
6. Continue until all 150 turns are complete.

**You can take breaks.** Your progress auto-saves. Use the **Previous Turn** button if you want to revise an earlier answer. The interface includes detailed guidelines with definitions and examples.
